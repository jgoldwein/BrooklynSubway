# Brooklyn Subway: start here

Specially Designed for Avi Yao

[Download the one-page packing sheet (PDF)](shipping/Brooklyn_Subway_Quick_Start.pdf)

These instructions match the current preview firmware, 0.2.77. Older releases
may work differently.

## Turn it on

1. Plug in the supplied **5 V power adapter**. A computer is not needed.
2. If the OLED asks you to join **BrooklynMap-Avi**, do the first-time Wi-Fi
   setup below. You do not need to wait for green lights before setting up Wi-Fi.
3. After connecting, watch for **SYSTEM, NETWORK, and MTA FEED to turn green**.
   Red and amber are normal while starting.

Each light gives a short readiness beep when it becomes green. You will also
hear the startup tune. Quiet hours or holding DIM during startup can mute sounds.
The station lights stay dark until train data is ready.

## First time: connect to home Wi-Fi

1. On your phone, open **Wi-Fi settings** and join **BrooklynMap-Avi**.
   If your phone says there is no internet, choose **Stay connected** or
   **Use without internet**.
2. Open **http://192.168.4.1/** if the setup page does not appear. Set a map
   password and save it somewhere safe. Then choose your home Wi-Fi, enter its
   Wi-Fi password, and tap **Connect to Wi-Fi**. A shared 2.4/5 GHz network works;
   the map needs its 2.4 GHz signal.
3. When the page says **You're connected!**, note the map's address. Rejoin your
   home Wi-Fi on your phone, wait about **10 seconds**, close the setup pop-up, and open that address in
   Safari, Chrome, or your usual browser. Bookmark it.

You only need to do this once, unless your home Wi-Fi changes. Next time, just
plug the map in. No app or account is needed.

More help: [Connect to Wi-Fi](AVI_USER_GUIDE.md#connect-to-wi-fi).

## Try these three things

- **Turn the knob:** browse the small OLED screens.
- **Press MODE:** show one subway line on the map. Turn the knob to choose a
  line. Press MODE again to bring back your saved lines.
- **Tap TEST:** watch each whole line turn red, green, then blue. It takes about
  15 seconds. Press POWER to stop a test.

## POWER has four steps

Starting with both displays on, each short press changes to:

1. Map lights off; OLED on.
2. Map lights on; OLED off.
3. Both off.
4. Both on again.

Hold POWER for **3 seconds** to restart. This keeps your saved settings.

BRIGHT and DIM work when **only one display is on**. They change that display.
When both displays are on or both are off, those buttons do nothing.

## What the map colors mean

The default colors are:

- **Yellow:** approaching a station.
- **Red:** a recent report places a train at a station.
- **Green:** leaving a station.
- **Blue:** uncertain or last-reported position.

The map follows MTA reports and estimates. It is not a GPS tracker. Several
trains can share one light, and updates arrive in batches.

## Leave the clock on

Turn to **Time & Date**. It stays there until you turn away. The time uses fixed
spacing and steady colons; seconds still change every second.

For a bedroom, turn the map off with POWER, then use DIM to lower the OLED.
Quiet hours can also dim the OLED, silence beeps, and turn off or dim the map.
