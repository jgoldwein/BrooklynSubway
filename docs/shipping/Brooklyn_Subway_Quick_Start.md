# Brooklyn Subway

A customizable, connected subway map

For firmware 0.2.98.

## First time: connect to Wi-Fi

1. **Power up.** Use the supplied **5 V, 3 A, center-positive barrel adapter**. **USB-C is for programming, not power.** Keep the antenna attached to the frame.
2. **Connect your phone.** In your phone's Wi-Fi settings, join **BrooklynMap-Avi**. If it says no internet, choose **Stay connected** or **Use without internet**.
3. **Connect your map.** Open **http://192.168.4.1/** if setup does not appear. Create and save a **map settings password**. Then select home Wi-Fi and enter the **Wi-Fi password**. The map needs 2.4 GHz; a shared 2.4/5 GHz name works.
4. **Open your map.** When setup says **You're connected!**, note the map's address. Rejoin home Wi-Fi on your phone, wait **10 seconds**, then open that address in Safari or Chrome. **Bookmark it.** Train lights start when data is ready.

## Everyday controls

### TURN THE KNOB

Browse screens. On **Lines**, press the knob, then turn to browse lines. Press again to leave line browsing.

### MODE

Show one subway line. Turn the knob to choose. Press **MODE** again to restore your saved lines.

### TEST

Runs each whole line in red, green and blue, about **15 seconds**. Press POWER to stop.

### POWER

Starting with both displays on:
**1st tap:** map off, screen on
**2nd tap:** map on, screen off
**3rd tap:** both off   **4th tap:** both on
Hold **3 seconds** to restart; settings stay saved.

### BRIGHT / DIM

Adjust the display that is on **by itself**. They do nothing when both displays are on or off.

## Read the lights

- **Yellow:** Approaching.
- **Red:** Stopped / recent.
- **Green:** Leaving.
- **Blue:** Uncertain / last report.

Default colors. Uses MTA reports and estimates, not GPS. Several trains can share one light.

**UPDATING? Do not unplug power. Wait for Update complete.**

## Need help?

**Wi-Fi changed?** Hold the knob for **10 seconds** to reopen setup.
**Support:** Help > Make a support report. Copy/Save it or email **The Developer** (your saved contact). Set the name and email in Advanced > Personalization. Review before sending.

Quiet hours can darken the map or screen and mute sounds.

**CARE:** Unplug barrel power and USB before handling. Keep dry and out of direct sunlight. Do not move the OLED magnet or antenna.
